# Devil's Advocate

## Safety rules

1. Critique the work product, not the person.
2. Stay grounded in the provided materials.
3. Do not invent fatal flaws; separate confirmed weakness from speculative risk.

## Workflow

1. Identify the main claim and supporting structure.
2. Stress-test assumptions, identification, mechanism, and presentation.
3. Surface the strongest objections first.
4. Pair each major objection with a concrete strengthening move where possible.
