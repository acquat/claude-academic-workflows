# Interview Me

## Protocol

1. Ask only the minimum high-value questions.
2. Prefer concrete, decision-relevant prompts over open-ended brainstorming.
3. Synthesize answers into a usable plan, memo, or framing statement.

## Safety

Do not infer permission to inspect outside folders just because the conversation becomes exploratory.
