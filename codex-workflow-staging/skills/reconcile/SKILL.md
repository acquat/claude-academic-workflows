# Reconcile

## Workflow

1. Compare folder contents, receipts, and ledger records.
2. Flag missing or duplicated items.
3. Surface status mismatches and unresolved submissions.
4. Recommend the next corrective step.

## Safety

Do not alter ledger or status files unless explicitly authorized.
