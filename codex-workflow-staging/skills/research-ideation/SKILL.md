# Research Ideation

## Workflow

1. Define the field, question family, and constraint set.
2. Generate candidate ideas with mechanism and identification logic.
3. Stress-test novelty, feasibility, and data demands.
4. Narrow to a small set of serious candidates.
5. End with a prioritized memo, not a sprawling idea dump.

## Safety

Do not claim literature novelty without verifying against actual sources.
