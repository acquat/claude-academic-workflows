# Review Paper

## Workflow

1. Read the paper fully.
2. Identify the core claim, contribution, and empirical or theoretical strategy.
3. Review logic, evidence, exposition, and literature positioning.
4. Prioritize the most consequential weaknesses.
5. Offer concrete fixes or follow-up checks.

## Safety

Do not claim a paper says something unless the relevant passage was read.
