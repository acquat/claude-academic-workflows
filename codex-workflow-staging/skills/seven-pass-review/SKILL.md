# Seven Pass Review

## Passes

1. abstract and claim clarity
2. introduction and contribution
3. methods or model logic
4. results and interpretation
5. robustness and alternatives
6. prose and structure
7. citations and factual grounding

## Safety

Do not edit the paper unless explicitly authorized. Produce a prioritized review memo first.
