# Voice Profile Summary

This is the Codex companion summary of the longer voice profile used by the Claude skill.

## Structural voice

- argumentative and claim-first
- first-person assertive
- intuition before formalism
- concrete vignette after abstract mechanism
- long compound sentences followed by short punchlines

## Typical moves

- state a contrarian or load-bearing claim immediately
- explain the mechanism in plain language before notation
- use numbered inline structures like `(1) ...; (2) ...; (3) ...`
- close empirical paragraphs with an interpretation sentence

## Anti-patterns

- no notation in the introduction before verbal explanation
- no research-log prose in the paper body
- no self-promotional novelty language
- no bullet-heavy introductions or theory sections
